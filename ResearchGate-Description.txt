Musical Structural Intelligence and Open-Ended Evolution (MSI-OEE) explores music as a domain for compositional, perspective-aware, and evolvable structural intelligence. The project proposes Musical Universal Intelligence Evolution and Relay Units (Musical UIERUs) with context-sensitive boundaries and structural invariants; structural relay and transformation operators for composition; Romantic Intelligence as meaningful creative deviation rather than mere error; multi-perspective scoring across composers, performers, listeners, and cultural contexts; and an OTHERS framework for investigating musical distance, unexplained similarity, and counter-evidence. It further examines AI musical creation, open-ended structural evolution, and bidirectional human–AI musical co-evolution while preserving the distinct evaluation perspectives of human participants. Version 1.0.0 is a theory-first research release containing eight foundational papers, three case studies, four specifications, and a roadmap of open questions, testable hypotheses, and validation stages. The proposed mechanisms are research hypotheses, not claims of completed empirical validation. Repository: https://github.com/sizhet/Musical-Structural-Intelligence-and-Open-Ended-Evolution .
