### Research position
- Established a distinction between **structural musical identity** and **context-dependent musical function**.
- Proposed **Romantic Intelligence** as a research construct concerning meaningful creative deviation, without assuming that novelty alone constitutes value.
- Introduced **perspective sovereignty** and explicit scoring governance to avoid collapsing diverse aesthetic judgments into one unqualified score.
- Distinguished **musical similarity**, **historical copying**, and **legal infringement** as different questions requiring different evidence.
- Framed **OTHERS** as an open residual and evidence-management mechanism rather than an automatic claim of discovery.
- Proposed bidirectional **human–AI musical co-evolution** without attributing unverified conscious experience to AI systems.
- Connected musical research questions to broader Structural Intelligence work while treating cross-domain transfer as a hypothesis to be tested.
