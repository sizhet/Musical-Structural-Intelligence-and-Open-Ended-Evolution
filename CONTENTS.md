Maintain the distinction between **conceptual proposal**, **illustrative example**, **testable hypothesis**, **empirical observation**, and **independently validated finding**. A musical similarity score is not a determination of copying, provenance, or copyright infringement. Functional AI musical preference is not evidence of subjective musical experience.

## 8. Bidirectional Research Relay

MSI-OEE is designed to exchange research ideas and *validated* structural findings with related work on Universal Intelligence Evolution and Relay Units (UIERU), Structural Intelligence Framework (SIF), Universal Control Planes and Scoring Folding (UCPSF), Subjective Structural Intelligence (SSI), Beyond Matching and Open Evidence Intelligence (BM-ODWEI), structural recursive self-improvement, and structural folding/unfolding.

**Relay principle:** Existing structural frameworks → musical hypotheses and tests → evidence and counter-evidence → candidate extensions to general structural intelligence. A music-domain result does not become a universal principle without cross-domain validation.
